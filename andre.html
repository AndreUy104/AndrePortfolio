How I got here

In senior high, I was headed toward medicine — until the sheer volume of textbooks and unfamiliar terminology scared me off. A fellow CFCVFB volunteer introduced me to programming during the COVID lockdowns, and we spent that time tutoring each other and grinding through freeCodeCamp courses.

Then he handed me something real: a bug in the brigade's own records system, the one tracking duties, fire responses, meetings, and training. Fixing it, and later building a feature on top of it, felt less like coding and more like something else entirely.

"It felt like diagnosing a patient — read the symptoms, trace the cause, find the cure. That's still what keeps debugging fun for me."
